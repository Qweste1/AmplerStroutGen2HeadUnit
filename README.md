# Ampler Strout Gen 2 Head Unit

I have a nearly working head unit, except that the display no longer works because the cable connecting the display to the main unit has failed.
I’m currently looking for ways to replace or repair the unit, and I thought I’d share some of my findings along the way in case they might be useful to others facing a similar issue.

## Removing the Unit

The unit is connected to the bike with three cables, as shown [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6S0qXE2csM8).
The main connector appears to be a **HIGO-B9-F** (male) and **HIGO-S9-F** (female).

## The Display

In my case, only the display backlight was working.
After removing the single screw and carefully peeling away some of the silicone, I was able to separate the display from the rest of the head unit.
Unfortunately, during the process I seem to have damaged the display even further. When I connect it now, it shows some strange/funky pixels. This may not be surprising, as I had already noticed some damage beforehand, and quite a bit of force was required to remove the silicone holding the display in place.
After removing the display, I also removed the remaining transparent plastic that holds the different layers of the display assembly together.
